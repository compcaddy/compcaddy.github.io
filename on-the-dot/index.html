On the Dot

Stop the timer. Exactly.

A retro neon timing game for iPhone and iPad.

Tap Start, count in your head, and tap Stop exactly on target. Clear 1.0 seconds and the next level asks for 1.00, then 1.000, all the way to seven decimal places. Take on 1, 5, 30 and 60-second targets, try Blind mode with the timer hidden, and climb the weekly Game Center leaderboards.
