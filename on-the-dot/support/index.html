Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email phil@claim727.com. I usually reply within a couple of days.

How do I play?

Pick a target (1, 5, 30 or 60 seconds), tap Start, and tap Stop when you think the time is exactly right. Stop on the target to clear the level and unlock the next, more precise one. Blind mode hides the running timer.

How do levels unlock?

Clearing a level unlocks the next level for that target and mode. To jump ahead, you can watch a short video for 25 attempts on a locked level, or buy Full Access to unlock everything.

What does Full Access include?

Full Access is a one-time purchase. It removes all ads and unlocks every level in both modes. It's not a subscription, and Family Sharing is supported.

I bought Full Access but it isn't showing

  • Open Settings → Restore Purchases in the app while signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.
  • Make sure you have an internet connection, then reopen the app.
  • Still stuck? Tap Settings → Purchase Help, or email me with the date of purchase.

Refunds

Apple handles all payments, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Video attempts

Watching a video gives you 25 attempts on the locked level you picked. Your remaining attempts are saved, even if you close the app. If no video is available, you get the attempts anyway.

Leaderboards and achievements

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings to post scores and earn achievements. Weekly leaderboards reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Ads and privacy choices

You can change ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you can update consent in the app under Settings → Privacy Choices. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Resetting progress

Settings → Clear all data resets your local results. Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple.
